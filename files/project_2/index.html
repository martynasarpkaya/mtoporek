About the project

As part of a healthcare sector initiative, I created comprehensive user documentation for prostate cancer treatment software, tailored specifically for medical personnel, there was major change in desing of interfaces.

🎯 Goals

  • Support safe use of the software in clinical conditions.
  • Ensure compliance with standards and medical requirements.
  • Facilitate training and onboarding for clinicians and other users.
  • Promote consistency of use across different clinical teams and environments.
  • Provide a basis for updates and change when the software goes outdated.

⏳ Process - new approch

  • Step-by-step instructions to guide clinicians through the software’s workflows
  • User interface overviews to help users navigate efficiently.
  • Medical process flow guidelines aligned with clinical practices.
  • Aligned documentation with risk management processes and regulatory requirements.
  • Collaborate with subject matter experts (SMEs), such as clinicians, risk managers, and regulatory specialists.
  • Design content for clinincal user
  • Use clear, unambiguous language to reduce the chance of misinterpretation in critical clinical setting.
