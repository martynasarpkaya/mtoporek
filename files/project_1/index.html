The Assembly (POB) project was developed entirely from scratch as a specialised work instruction for cleanroom operations. The client was looking for an alternative to the existing solution in order to improve clarity and usability.

Goals

The primary goals were to:

  • Create a clear, easy-to-understand work instruction for cleanroom.
  • Make the guide usable by employees regardless of language proficiency.
  • Replace the existing method, which involved showing the product assembly by a person and giving verbal instructions, with a more useful and accessible alternative in the form of illustrated instructions.
  • Improve onboarding speed and reduce operational errors.

Process

How new approch was developed:

  • Built the guide from scratch as a dedicated visual instruction.
  • Prioritised illustrations and limited step-by-step instructions.
  • Collaborated closely with a illustrator to create the drawings.
  • Made chages to content and illistrations based on feedback from both myself and the client to ensure clarity and correctness.
  • Delivered a user-friendly, visual-first guide tailored specifically for cleanroom.
