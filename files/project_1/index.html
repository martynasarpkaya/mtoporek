About the project

The Assembly (POB) project was developed entirely from scratch as a specialised work instruction for cleanroom operations. The client was looking for an alternative to the existing solution in order to improve clarity and usability.

👤 Role

Led the project, developing pilot instructions, designing templates, and content, guiding the work of the illustrator to create accurate and effective drawings.

⏳ Process

  • Built the guide from scratch as dedicated visual instructions.
  • Use of illustrations and limited step-by-step instructions.
  • Collaborate closely with an illustrator to create the drawings.
  • Made chages to content and illustrations based on feedback from both myself and the client to ensure clarity and correctness.
  • Delivered a user-friendly, visual-first guide tailored specifically for cleanroom.

🏆 Results

  • Useable guide for colleagues with english as a second language.
  • Start of long-term cooperation with the client after successful verification.

⚙️ Tools and methods

  • InDesign
  • Internal standards, terminology and Simplified Technical English (STE)
