Project

The Assembly (POB) project was developed completely from scratch as a dedicated work instruction for cleanroom operations. The client sought an alternative to their existing solution to improve clarity and usability.

Challenges

Main challenge:

  • Design a guide easily understood by employees with diverse backgrounds and varying language skills.
  • Reduce reliance on text-heavy materials that limited accessibility and slowed learning.

Goals

The primary goals were to:

  • Make instructions accessible to employees regardless of language proficiency.
  • Increase clarity and usability compared to the previous solution.
  • Support faster onboarding and reduce operational errors.

Process

Approach taken:

  • Prioritised visuals over text to make steps immediately clear.
  • Created illustrations collaboratively with a colleague, and iterated based on my and the client's feedback.
  • Minimised written instructions; where text was necessary, kept it concise and paired it with clear visuals.
