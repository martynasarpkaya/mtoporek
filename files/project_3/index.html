About the project

Customer News documentation was prepared for sales teams and their partners. It contains key information about new products and technical updates.

👤 Role

Collaborated with the product engineer and verified content with the sales department to prepare news updates. Simplified and translated technical content into clear, accessible language.

⏳ Process

  • Gathered product and technical update details from product engineers.
  • Use of illustrations and limited step-by-step instructions.
  • Structured the content to balance technical accuracy with sales usability.
  • Included clear explanations of mechanisms and operational differences.

🏆 Results

  • Delivered concise and relevant product and technical updates.
  • Clarified how this mechanism works in practice.
  • Highlighted differences in operation between product versions or updates.
  • Provided to the sales teams an accessible reference material for their clients.

⚙️ Tools and methods

  • Cosima CMS
  • Internal style guide, clear content creation using templates
